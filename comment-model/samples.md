> 
> - Self hosted on a VPS?
> - Managed infrastructure like AWS, Fly, Render, Digital Ocean App Platform, GCP?
> - Full-stack platform like Vercel, Firebase, Supabase?

**Base Qwen2.5-1.5B** : Great topic! As someone who's also a solo developer managing multiple projects, especially within a small team context, what strategies do you find most effective for maintaining efficiency and quality while keeping costs down? Any tools or platforms you've found particularly useful?

**Fine-tuned (LoRA)** : I host everything myself.

10 years ago when I was just starting out it was a big deal to run servers yourself. Now, with cloud providers offering self-hosting options (e.g. Heroku), it's easy enough for almost anyone to do it without much overhead. Plus, there's something comforting about having control over all aspects of your app, from server setup to DNS management to database management.

It also lets me be more flexible and not have to rely on any particular vendor.

*Vrai commentaire HN* : Self-hosted on a VPS is the first option for me, it's cheap and you have total control on everything. It's valid in my case because I know my way through infrastructure and configuration, so a panel isn't too much gain.

You sometimes can even get those for free - https://free-for.dev/#/?id=major-cloud-providers

For example, I have some (really) free instances on Oracle Cloud that I host pages, experiments, and so many things.
